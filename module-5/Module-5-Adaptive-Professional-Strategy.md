# Module 5: My Adaptive Professional Strategy

This activity turns the four practices of the Adaptive Professional (Continuous Learning, Strategic Networking, Personal Brand and Visibility, and Reflective Practice) into specific commitments I'll carry beyond this course. Each one connects to my [Integrated Career-Degree Roadmap](Module-5-Integrated-Career-Degree-Roadmap.md) and my [Contingency Plans](Module-5-Contingency-Plans.md).

## Step 2 — My Adaptive Professional Pledge

*To myself, ten years from now:*

I'm writing this as a senior who switched into Applied AI late, builds AI tools and websites for clients, and is launching a food and beverage brand. I'm committing to four habits: learning something new every week, building real relationships instead of working alone, showing my work instead of hiding it, and stopping every week to ask whether I'm still headed where I want to go.

This matters to me because both of my fields change fast. The AI tools I'm learning this year might be outdated in three, and the food industry keeps changing what customers want. What keeps me useful is the habit of learning and adjusting, not any single skill.

The biggest obstacle I see is myself. I take on too much, and when something new comes along I add it instead of dropping something. To fight that, my Sunday review has one rule: before I add anything, I name one thing I'm pausing.

I'll hold myself accountable in public. My learning log, case studies, and plan updates will live in my GitHub portfolio, where anyone can see whether I kept up. I'll also review my numbers with a mentor at least once a semester.

Ten years from now, I want to be running my own food-tech company, or be a technical leader who makes real decisions, known for understanding both the science and the software. If I kept these promises, you'll have the network, portfolio, and habits to prove it.
