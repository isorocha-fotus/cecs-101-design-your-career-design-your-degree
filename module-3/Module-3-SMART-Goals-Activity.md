# SMART Goals Activity

Vague intentions do not produce results. This activity moves you from intention to commitment — from "I hope to" into "I will, by when, measured how." You will write a minimum of 5 SMART Goals across academic, career, and personal development categories.

---

## Step 1 — Review Your POV Statement

Reread your POV Statement. Your goals should move you in the direction defined by your POV Statement. If they do not connect, something is misaligned.

**My POV Statement (for reference):**
> I am a senior who started in food science, switched into Applied AI, and is building my own food and beverage brand and client business while finishing my degree. I need a path that lets me combine technology, science, and business so I can build products that people love, because I've learned that I'm most motivated and most capable when I take an idea all the way from the lab or the laptop to a real customer.

---

## Step 2 — Brainstorm Goal Areas

Before writing formal SMART goals, brainstorm freely. For each category below, list 3–4 things you want to accomplish in the next 1–4 years. Do not filter yourself in this step — just generate ideas.

**Academic** *(courses, GPA, relationships with faculty, study habits...)*
- Finish Fall 2026 courses (CECS 399, AI 102, AI 201) with strong grades
- Complete AI 202, AI 301, and AI 302 in Spring 2027
- Keep my GPA above 3.5 through graduation
- Graduate in Fall 2027 with the Food Science minor finished

**Career** *(internships, skills, certifications, networking...)*
- Apply to at least 10 internships by January 2027
- Complete the CECS 375 internship in Summer 2027
- Finish 5 more client projects, at least 3 of them paid
- Build a tool for small food producers as my capstone project

**Personal Development** *(campus involvement, health, community, leadership...)*
- Protect one full day off each week to avoid burnout
- Join a student organization related to food science or AI
- Talk to professionals about their careers every semester
- Learn to say no to projects that don't fit my plan

---

## Step 3 — Select and Write 5 SMART Goals

Choose at least 5 goals from your brainstorm — at minimum, include:
- 2 Academic Goals (at least one short-term and one mid-term)
- 2 Career Goals (at least one short-term and one mid-term)
- 1 Personal Development Goal

For each goal, complete the following SMART evaluation.

### Goal 1 (Academic — Short-Term)

**Goal Statement:**
Complete all of my Fall 2026 courses (including CECS 399, AI 102, and AI 201) with a B or higher in each by the end of the semester in December 2026.

**S — Specific:** What exactly will I do or achieve?
I will finish every Fall 2026 course with at least a B, with special attention to CECS 399 (my capstone) and my two AI major courses.

**M — Measurable:** How will I track progress?
I will check my grades on Canvas after each exam or major assignment and keep a simple list of where each course stands. Success is a B or higher in every course on my final grade report.

**A — Achievable:** Why is this realistic given my current situation?
My current GPA is 3.64, and I already earned a B in AI 101. My courses this semester build directly on what I'm already doing outside of class.

**R — Relevant:** How does this connect to my POV Statement and career direction?
The major requires at least a C, but strong grades in these courses are what I need to be credible when I apply for internships and show employers I have real AI skills.

**T — Time-Bound:** By when will I achieve this?
By the final grade posting in December 2026.

**Immediate Next Action:** What is one concrete step I can take THIS WEEK?
Write out every due date for my courses through the end of the semester in one calendar and block study time for the next two weeks.

### Goal 2 (Academic — Mid-Term)

**Goal Statement:**
Complete AI 202, AI 301, and AI 302 in Spring 2027 with a B or higher in each and keep my cumulative GPA at or above 3.5 by the end of May 2027.

**S — Specific:** What exactly will I do or achieve?
I will take the three core AI courses in Spring 2027 and earn a B or better in each, while keeping my overall GPA above 3.5.

**M — Measurable:** How will I track progress?
I will track my course grades monthly and compare my GPA on my degree audit at the end of the semester.

**A — Achievable:** Why is this realistic given my current situation?
I've planned my schedule so these three major courses are together, and my prerequisites will be finished by then. My current GPA is above the goal.

**R — Relevant:** How does this connect to my POV Statement and career direction?
These are the courses that give me the technical foundation for building AI tools, which is the technology half of my POV Statement.

**T — Time-Bound:** By when will I achieve this?
By the end of the Spring 2027 semester (May 2027).

**Immediate Next Action:** What is one concrete step I can take THIS WEEK?
Meet with my advisor or check the course schedule to confirm AI 202, 301, and 302 will be offered together in Spring 2027 and that I'm registered when registration opens.

### Goal 3 (Career — Short-Term)

**Goal Statement:**
Apply to at least 10 internships in AI, tech, food, or product development by January 31, 2027, and secure one for Summer 2027.

**S — Specific:** What exactly will I do or achieve?
I will send at least 10 internship applications to companies in AI, technology, food and beverage, or product development, and aim to receive at least one offer for Summer 2027 that counts for CECS 375.

**M — Measurable:** How will I track progress?
I will keep a spreadsheet of each application with its date, status, and follow-up. Success is 10 applications submitted by the deadline and at least one offer.

**A — Achievable:** Why is this realistic given my current situation?
I have real client projects and my own brand to show in a portfolio, so my experience is concrete even though I haven't had a formal internship. Applying to 10 gives me a reasonable chance of at least one offer.

**R — Relevant:** How does this connect to my POV Statement and career direction?
An internship is the missing piece in my SWOT and the best way to learn how companies build products, which supports my goal of taking an idea all the way to a customer.

**T — Time-Bound:** By when will I achieve this?
Applications by January 31, 2027, with an offer by April 2027.

**Immediate Next Action:** What is one concrete step I can take THIS WEEK?
Update my resume and portfolio, then make a list of 15 companies or programs that fit.

### Goal 4 (Career — Mid-Term)

**Goal Statement:**
Complete a CECS 375 internship and finish 5 more client projects, at least 3 of them paid, by the end of Summer 2027.

**S — Specific:** What exactly will I do or achieve?
I will complete an internship for CECS 375 credit while continuing my own business, and deliver five more client projects (websites or AI tools), with at least three paid.

**M — Measurable:** How will I track progress?
I will count completed projects in my portfolio and track paid work in a simple log. Success is 5 delivered projects, 3 or more paid, and a passing grade for the internship.

**A — Achievable:** Why is this realistic given my current situation?
I'm already doing client work, so this is a continuation, not a new activity. Summer has no other classes besides one elective, so I'll have time to do both.

**R — Relevant:** How does this connect to my POV Statement and career direction?
This puts both parts of my POV Statement into action at once: professional experience inside a company and building real products for real customers on my own.

**T — Time-Bound:** By when will I achieve this?
By the end of August 2027.

**Immediate Next Action:** What is one concrete step I can take THIS WEEK?
List my current and potential clients and identify which projects I can finish and show first.

---

### Goal 5 (Personal Development)

**Goal Statement:**
Take one full day off from school and work every week for the rest of this semester (through December 2026) so that I avoid burnout while carrying school, client work, and my own brand.

**S — Specific:** What exactly will I do or achieve?
I will pick one day each week with no classwork, client work, or business tasks, and protect it.

**M — Measurable:** How will I track progress?
I will mark each week on my calendar as "kept" or "missed." Success is keeping the day off in at least 12 of the remaining weeks of the semester.

**A — Achievable:** Why is this realistic given my current situation?
It requires planning my work ahead, which I already do, and I can reschedule the day within the week if I have to.

**R — Relevant:** How does this connect to my POV Statement and career direction?
My SWOT and empathy map both showed that I overcommit and get tired. Staying healthy is what lets me keep building the things my POV Statement is about.

**T — Time-Bound:** By when will I achieve this?
Every week through the end of the Fall 2026 semester in December 2026.

**Immediate Next Action:** What is one concrete step I can take THIS WEEK?
Choose which day of the week will be my day off and put it on my calendar as a recurring event.

## Step 5 — Reflect

Write a short reflection (6–10 sentences):

- Which goal feels most urgent and why?
- Which goal feels most challenging and why?
- How do your goals collectively reflect your POV Statement?
- What surprised you about this goal-setting process?

**My Reflection:**
The most urgent goal is the internship goal, because the application deadline is January 31 and the internship is the biggest gap in my experience. The most challenging goal is probably the weekly day off, which sounds simple but goes against my habit of starting more projects than I have time for. My goals together follow my POV Statement: the academic goals build the technical foundation, the career goals give me real-world experience and customers, and the personal goal protects my ability to keep going. What surprised me was how much of the plan depends on managing my time rather than on my ability. Writing measurable targets also made the plan feel more real, because I can now tell whether I'm on track. I'll check this list at the end of each semester and adjust it as my situation changes.
