# SMART Goals Activity

Vague intentions do not produce results. This activity moves you from intention to commitment — from "I hope to" into "I will, by when, measured how." You will write a minimum of 5 SMART Goals across academic, career, and personal development categories.

---

## Step 1 — Review Your POV Statement

Reread your POV Statement. Your goals should move you in the direction defined by your POV Statement. If they do not connect, something is misaligned.

**My POV Statement (for reference):**
I am someone who wants to understand people and use technology, science, business, and creativity to make things that people love. I need an education that gives me the skills, experiences, relationships, and freedom to turn ideas into real products instead of limiting myself to one traditional path. Because I have learned that my potential is not limited by a lack of ideas or ambition, but by how well I control my attention and consistently turn those ideas into action.

---

## Step 2 — Brainstorm Goal Areas

Before writing formal SMART goals, brainstorm freely. For each category below, list 3–4 things you want to accomplish in the next 1–4 years. Do not filter yourself in this step — just generate ideas.

**Academic** *(courses, GPA, relationships with faculty, study habits...)*
- Improve my ability to focus and complete work without constantly getting distracted.
- Continue developing my technical skills in AI, programming, and data.
- Build stronger relationships with professors and people who have knowledge I can learn from.
- Create a clear degree path that supports AI, product development, and entrepreneurship.

**Career** *(internships, skills, certifications, networking...)*
- Get an internship related to AI, food, consumer products, data, or product development.
- Build relationships with professionals who could become mentors, collaborators, or resources when developing products.
- Gain real experience developing and testing products instead of only learning about them.
- Build a portfolio that shows my technical skills, ideas, and ability to solve real problems.

**Personal Development** *(campus involvement, health, community, leadership...)*
- Gain better control over my phone and attention.
- Become more consistent instead of relying on pressure or motivation.
- Surround myself with more ambitious, creative, and motivated people.
- Become better at turning ideas into action and actually finishing what I start.

---

## Step 3 — Select and Write 5 SMART Goals

Choose at least 5 goals from your brainstorm — at minimum, include:
- 2 Academic Goals (at least one short-term and one mid-term)
- 2 Career Goals (at least one short-term and one mid-term)
- 1 Personal Development Goal

For each goal, complete the following SMART evaluation.

### Goal 1 (Academic — Short-Term)

**Goal Statement:**
By the end of Fall 2026, I will create and stick with a more consistent routine by completing at least two phone-free 50-minute focused work sessions five days per week. I will also build and manage 3 websites/tools I create with AI for 3 clients (I am at 1 already).

**S — Specific:** What exactly will I do or achieve?
I will schedule focused work sessions where my phone is away and I work only on my work and school.

**M — Measurable:** How will I track progress? How will I know I succeeded?
I will track my focus sessions each week and whether I submit my assignments on time and make genuine progress on my projects.

**A — Achievable:** Why is this realistic given my current situation?
Two focused sessions per day is realistic with my current schedule and does not require me to completely change how I spend my day.

**R — Relevant:** How does this connect to my POV Statement and career direction?
Improving my attention directly addresses one of the biggest weaknesses I found in my SWOT Analysis and Empathy Map.

**T — Time-Bound:** By when will I achieve this? What is my deadline?
I will maintain this routine through the end of the Fall 2026 semester.

**Immediate Next Action:** What is one concrete step I can take THIS WEEK toward this goal?
This week, I will choose two specific times each weekday for focused work and put my phone somewhere I cannot reach it during those sessions.

### Goal 5 (Personal Development)

**Goal Statement:**
By December 1, 2026, I will improve my ability to control my attention by reducing unnecessary phone use by at least 30% and using that time more intentionally toward school, my personal AI and website work, and other long-term goals.

**S — Specific:** What exactly will I do or achieve?
I will reduce time spent on meaningless phone distractions and create more focused time for school, client projects, learning, and personal development.

**M — Measurable:** How will I track progress? How will I know I succeeded?
I will track my weekly Screen Time and maintain at least a 30% reduction from my current average for four consecutive weeks.

**A — Achievable:** Why is this realistic given my current situation?
This is realistic because I do not need to completely stop using my phone. I only need to become more intentional about when I use it and what deserves my attention.

**R — Relevant:** How does this connect to my POV Statement and career direction?
Better control of my attention will make me more effective in school, help me grow my work building websites and AI tools for companies, and allow me to make more consistent progress toward my long-term goals.

**T — Time-Bound:** By when will I achieve this? What is my deadline?
I will reach this goal by December 1, 2026 and maintain the reduction for at least four consecutive weeks.

**Immediate Next Action:** What is one concrete step I can take THIS WEEK toward this goal?
This week, I will record my current Screen Time average, turn off unnecessary notifications, and create specific phone-free work periods for school and company projects.

## Step 5 — Reflect

Write a short reflection (6–10 sentences):

- Which goal feels most urgent and why?
- Which goal feels most challenging and why?
- How do your goals collectively reflect your POV Statement?
- What surprised you about this goal-setting process?

**My Reflection:**
The goal that feels most urgent is improving my control over my attention because it affects almost every other goal I have. If I continue letting distractions take up too much of my time, having ambitious goals will not matter if I do not consistently work toward them. The most challenging goal will probably be completing a meaningful internship or product-development experience because it requires me to improve my skills, compete for opportunities, and put myself in situations I have not experienced before. All of these goals connect to my POV Statement because they help me gain the skills, relationships, and experience I need to eventually make things that people love. They also allow me to explore AI, science, business, and product development without forcing myself into only one path. What surprised me most was that many of my goals are not about figuring out what I want because I already have a good understanding of that. They are more about becoming disciplined enough to consistently turn what I want into action.
